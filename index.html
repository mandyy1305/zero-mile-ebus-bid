3. Tender-rule checklist (confirm each in the RFP)

A. Commercial & pricing

  • ☐ Confirm the exact billing unit and definition of “kilometre” — GPS/telematics-logged km, depot-run vs scheduled km, and rounding rules. [RFP — Definitions & payment schedule]
  • ☐ Confirm the per-km ceiling (L1) and whether AC/non-AC and 12 m/9 m variants are priced in separate lots. [Schedule of rates — price bid format]
  • ☐ Confirm the assured/guaranteed monthly km per bus and the minimum-payment percentage if actual km fall short (e.g., 90% of assured km). [Service Levels — assured km]
  • ☐ Confirm the annual escalation / indexation formula (electricity tariff, wage/CPI, R&M inflation) and the year it first applies. [Price escalation clause]
  • ☐ Confirm which subsidy applies (FAME-II / PM-eBus Sewa ₹24/km for 12 m) and whether it is netted off the bid or paid separately. [Subsidy / op-support clause]
  • ☐ Confirm GST applicability and rate on the per-km service, plus treatment of input tax credit. [Taxes & duties clause]
  • ☐ Confirm billing cycle, invoice-to-payment days, and interest on delayed payment. [Payment terms]
  • ☐ Confirm deductions & penalties — availability/uptime penalty, breakdown deductions, missed-trip LD. [Penalty / LD schedule]

B. Contract & payment security

  • ☐ Confirm contract duration (typically 12 years) and termination provisions including termination-for-convenience compensation. [Term & termination]
  • ☐ Confirm the payment security mechanism — escrow, bank guarantee, or sovereign PSM (as in PM-eBus Sewa's ₹3,435 Cr guarantee). [Payment Security Mechanism]
  • ☐ Confirm Performance Security and Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) amounts, form, validity and interest. [EMD / PBG]
  • ☐ Confirm change-in-law and force-majeure provisions and their compensation treatment. [Change-in-law / FM]

C. Operations & service levels

  • ☐ Confirm minimum uptime/availability (e.g., ≥95%) and how an “available” bus is defined. [Service Levels]
  • ☐ Confirm daily deployment plan, routes, headways, first/last trip timings and dead-mileage allowances. [Route/duty schedule]
  • ☐ Confirm fare-revenue handling — in GCC the authority typically retains fares; verify revenue-risk allocation. [GCC model / revenue]
  • ☐ Confirm dead km from depot to route origin is payable and its cap. [Dead km clause]

D. Infrastructure & energy

  • ☐ Confirm who provides depot land, the power connection, and the number/type of chargers; who bears single-point connection cost. [Depot & charging]
  • ☐ Confirm the applicable electricity tariff category and whether tariff escalation is passed through. [Energy clause]
  • ☐ Confirm battery State-of-Health (SOH) guarantee, replacement obligation and end-of-term residual value. [Battery SOH]

E. Technical specifications

  • ☐ Confirm bus spec — range, battery kWh, CMVR/AIS-153 homologation, AC/non-AC, low-floor, CCTV, ITS/live tracking, panic buttons, accessibility. [Technical spec]
  • ☐ Confirm vehicle approvals — ARAI/ICAT certification, registration, MVI, permits and any road-tax exemptions. [Approvals]

F. Financial eligibility & bid submission

  • ☐ Confirm eligibility — net worth, turnover, consortium/JV rules, minimum fleet size and operating experience. [Qualification criteria (technical bid)]
  • ☐ Confirm bid submission format — two-envelope (technical + financial), EMD and bid validity period, online/offline mode and deadline. [Bid documents]
  • ☐ Confirm whether 50 buses form one lot or several, minimum allotment per operator, and single vs multiple-operator award. [Lotting]
  • ☐ Confirm any local-content / Phased Manufacturing Programme (PMP) compliance for batteries/cells. [PMP / localisation]

G. Legal & exit

  • ☐ Confirm dispute resolution — arbitration venue, governing law and jurisdiction (Nagpur), plus the escalation ladder. [Dispute resolution]
  • ☐ Confirm assignment, change-of-control and subcontracting restrictions. [Assignment]
  • ☐ Confirm insurance requirements — comprehensive, third-party, passenger liability, deductibles and who pays claims. [Insurance]
  • ☐ Confirm liquidated damages and any incentive/bonus for high uptime. [LD / incentives]
  • ☐ Confirm data sharing & reporting — telematics feed to the authority's command-and-control centre and MIS frequency. [Data & reporting]
  • ☐ Confirm hand-back condition of buses and chargers at contract end. [Hand-back]
